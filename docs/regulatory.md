# Regulatory stance

> [Docs](README.md) › Regulatory · Up: [Overview](overview.md) · Related: [Upgrade authority](upgrade-authority.md), [Custody](custody.md), [Devnet](devnet.md)

Kadex combines fractionalised graded cards with a perpetual contract on a card index. Securities, derivatives, and gambling regimes may overlap. The team declares this question; it has not resolved it.

Kadex does not claim that card fractions, the index instrument, CLP shares, or perpetual positions fall outside any of those regimes. It does not claim that the prototype is approved for real-money trading or that a working program settles its legal status. This statement is not legal advice.

Before accepting real funds, the team would need legal review of the instruments and each intended place of operation. That review would need to address who may offer and use the market, whether registration or licensing applies, custody and redemption of cards and fractions, and the disclosures and controls needed for leveraged trading and pool losses. The team would also need to resolve the upgrade and admin-key design before real value is held.

Devnet assets hold no real value. Kadex has not been deployed to devnet; the prepared devnet flow uses demonstration assets and does not buy or vault the ten cards in the proposed launch basket.
